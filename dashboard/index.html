How the radar prices a launch

One function scores every row. No protocol name is special-cased.
implied = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × chains

Comparables

The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the token-trading protocols in the same category. A category needs at least 3 peers to price itself; thinner categories fall back to the global median over all peers. The multiple is clamped to 0.01–2. Peers are token-trading protocols outside the excluded categories.

Corrections

Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and half-weighted. Maturity rises with the log of the days since listing (unknown age counts as 365 days), from 0.7 to 1.0. Each chain beyond the first adds 5%, capped at four extra chains. Every input is a figure the snapshot carries.

Scope

The board ranks every launch candidate: a protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product. The snapshot's watchlist is the floor and is covered in full; the rows below the floor are the early radar. Implied value is a model, not a price, and no token is promised.
